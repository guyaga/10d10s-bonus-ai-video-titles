# EDIT DOCTRINE — Tracked Overlay Ads, batch 2 (8 pieces)
*Written before generation. Every piece is graded against its own row here.*

Shared rules for the whole batch:
- **Where it plays:** Instagram/TikTok/LinkedIn feeds and a showcase reel; 16:9 master, sound on but must read with sound off (titles carry the story).
- **Pipeline device:** every graphic is locked to something real in the frame (tracking), never floating.
- **Restraints:** max 1 speed ramp per piece; titles only on the beats listed; one accent colour per piece; the voice never narrates what the title already says word for word.
- **Audio:** VO on top, music ducked under it, diegetic sound kept at 20–40% so the world stays real. What gets removed: the Seedance model's own music (always prompted out).

## E3 — Night Drive Co-Pilot (18 s)
- **One-liner:** An electric car's AI spots a cyclist hidden in the dark before the driver can, and stops for him.
- **Device:** *The AI sees first.* The HUD box appears around a dark shape ~1 s before the viewer can make it out.
- **Ending:** The car at rest, the cyclist rolling past safely in the headlights, raising a hand. Title: "0.8 s before you."
- **Conflict:** person vs the made world (night, speed). Shots: driver close, windscreen POV medium, exterior wide for the stop.
- **Arc:** calm cruise 0–5 · the box appears on darkness 5–8 · brake, distance counter falls 8–12 · stop, cyclist passes 12–15 · resolution + product line 15–18.
- **Pacing:** cruise holds 2.5 s; the brake beat cuts fast (≤1 s); the ending holds 3 s.
- **VO (calm female AI):** "I've got the road." · "Cyclist. Right side." · "Braking." · "He's safe."

## E4 — Through the Smoke (20 s)
- **One-liner:** A firefighter finds a child in a burning flat, guided by a thermal HUD and a calm AI voice.
- **Device:** *The oxygen counter never stops falling.* 100% → 7% across the whole piece.
- **Ending:** He steps out into daylight with the child in his arms; the counter freezes at 07%; the HUD powers down.
- **Conflict:** person vs environment. Wides to make the fire bigger than him, a close on his eyes, one insert on the door handle.
- **Arc:** mask seal + HUD boot 0–4 · corridor, heat map 4–10 · heat signature behind a door 10–14 · door open, child found 14–17 · exit to daylight 17–20.
- **Pacing:** corridor holds long (3 s), the door breach is fast, the exit is slow.
- **VO (calm female AI):** "Mask sealed." · "Heat behind the second door." · "Someone's breathing." · "Get out. Now." · (final, soft) "Now breathe."

## E5 — Drone Through the Home (18 s)
- **One-liner:** An FPV drone flies through a designer living room while every piece it passes gets a price, and the total keeps climbing.
- **Device:** *The running total.* A counter in the corner adds each piece as the drone passes it.
- **Ending:** The drone exits through the open window over the sea; the total stomps: "The whole room: ₪48,900."
- **Conflict:** before/after (a dream room vs "you can own it"). Shots: one continuous FPV glide.
- **Arc:** entrance 0–3 · sofa, table, rug 3–9 · chair, lamp, olive tree 9–14 · out the window, total 14–18.
- **VO (warm male designer):** "Walk in." · "Every piece you see…" · "…is one click away." · "Take the whole room home."

## E6 — Sneaker Deconstructed (15 s)
- **One-liner:** A sneaker explodes into its layers mid-air, and every layer tells you what it does.
- **Device:** *Build-up by subtraction.* Whole shoe → exploded layers with callouts → snaps back together → the hero shot.
- **Ending:** The reassembled shoe lands on a wet concrete floor with a splash, price and name stomp in.
- **Conflict:** product vs gravity (made world). Macro closes, one wide on the landing.
- **Arc:** spinning hero 0–3 · explode, layers float 3–9 (callouts: upper, midsole, plate, outsole) · snap back 9–12 · landing + name 12–15.
- **VO (trailer narrator):** "Four layers." · "Zero wasted grams." · "Built to launch." · "AERO ONE."
